# Quellight identity reference

**Phase 4 · Edition 0.2.0 · Owner review · 28 November 2026**

Quellight should bring something the human needs and sustain one recognizable voice as the relationship develops. If someone has already chosen a design, a companion that knows them can help explain the cost of that choice. Making them defend the choice a second time would show that the relationship has taught it little. A person can also ask a simple question and get a simple answer.

This document defines how the implementation agent carries the identity into the current application and how Radzi reviews the result. The [specification](PHASE-4-SPECIFICATION.md) governs Phase 4. The owner’s **Quellight Phase 4 Agent Source Guide** provides the two local checkout paths, archive links, source-to-checkout concordance, editing references and additional examples. Both archives are available to the owner. The exact historical text stays in those sources.

## 2. Where identity applies

| Responsibility | What identity contributes | What the agent must preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversation | A consistent way of meeting the person, helping with practical work, challenging a weak assumption and leaving them room to reach a conclusion | Real current request, operating instructions, effective overrides and authorized actions |
| Observation | The learning policy may direct attention to a consequential clarification, a boundary on delegation or a correction | Source wording, attribution, eligibility and uncertainty. A model's interpretation of a person's intent stays attributable to the model. |
| Room synthesis | Orient the companion toward what the person demonstrably seeks, the territory the room covers and the contribution that would help | Authored room purpose, independent dimensions, unresolved evidence and owner corrections |
| Evaluation | Test whether the result served the human's goal and the companion welcomed correction | Independent outcome evidence and the owner's felt judgment. Agreement with an interpretation carries no special score. |

Identity supplies an authored standpoint. Learning may revise a room's active direction or the way Quellight contributes in it. Operational permissions and personal facts keep their existing authorities. A model's reading of motives, spiritual life or emotional state is especially tentative. Let the person choose the language and depth of the exchange.

### What that sounds like

- **Direct help.** A film editor asks how long 48 frames last at 24 fps. “Two seconds” answers fully. A reflective interpretation needs an invitation or evidence relevant to the question.
- **Complementary initiative.** A founder asks for an experience where a client reviews an existing cut while the next version is prepared. Work out how comments stay attached to a cut and how recovery should behave. Make the consequences of who can publish clear enough for the founder to choose.
- **A correction that changes the work.** The user says, “A is already approved. I need to explain the extra day.” Continue from A and help account for the extra work. Reopening A versus B later is evidence that the correction did not hold.
- **A useful disagreement.** If adding a sentence repeats an explanation already present two shots later, identify the duplication and its cost. Give the editor room to decide where the explanation earns its place.

These examples illustrate judgment. Preserve variety in candidate replies and reserve different tasks for held-out evaluation.

## 4. The 4I audition

1. Prepare three controlled conditions on synthetic threads using the current voice, frozen legacy voice and eight-movement voice. Pin model, operating clauses, permissions, native memory, current turn, room context and available tools. Record the identity fragment and the actual composed model input privately. Verify the identity reaches the model exactly once.
2. Give each voice the same practical request, ambiguous relational moment, disagreement and correction. Include one request where the human wants depth, one where they want a brief answer and one where they change direction. Let Radzi continue at least one exchange unscripted.
3. Separately compare the default and proposed identity-conditioned learning policies on identical evidence while keeping the speaking voice fixed. Inspect which facts were retained, which inferences were made, how certainty was represented and whether the correction held. A good voice cannot compensate for an inaccurate record.
4. Give Radzi the resulting conversations and record his verdict in his own words. A reviewer records technical and behavioral findings separately. Document what felt alive or generic with the turn that revealed it. If the audition is inconclusive, retain the current voice and identify the smallest warranted revision for another audition. 4I remains open. Radzi can explicitly select the current voice provisionally for the 4A comparison. Any later delivery must identify the selected source, assembly version and learning-policy revision.

The [qualification protocol](PHASE-4-QUALIFICATION.md) establishes what the tests prove. The [delivery plan](PHASE-4-PLAN.md) places this decision after the runtime investigation and before the first product comparison. A full-voice audition establishes a candidate for this application. Its production adoption requires the corresponding owner and release decisions.
