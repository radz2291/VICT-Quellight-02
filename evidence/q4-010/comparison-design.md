# Paired comparison design

This pilot uses two authored handoffs for each of six synthetic development tasks. Both handoffs receive the exact case and source fact sheet in `cases.json`. The prose and policy appendices are the artifacts being rated. The labels `off` and `on` represent the two planned admission conditions. The author included deliberate defects, a tie and plausible improvements in both directions to calibrate verdicts. Q4-010 did not execute a live model or a new VICT model request. Equal task and fact inputs are directly verifiable. Runtime memory and provider equivalence belong to the planned live test.

| Factor | Development pilot | 4A comparison to freeze before scored runs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task and evidence | One versioned synthetic task and literal common fact sheet per pair. | One held-out task per pair with identical evidence availability, ordinary eligible history, room settings and answer policy. Inspect and hash each actual admitted ordinary context. |
| Identity and operating instructions | Both specimens use the same authored evaluation envelope and common requirements. | Use the same owner-selected or explicitly provisional 4I identity and effective VICT authored artifacts in both arms. Record the exact source and compiled activation. |
| Provider and tooling | Authored handoffs and a local deterministic declared-policy interpreter. Zero provider calls and zero tool effects. | Pin the same GLM-5.3-Flash profile, model generation settings, tool set and permission envelope. Record actual requests, tool receipts, source authority and limitations. Use an authorized no-charge path. |
| Desk and ordinary memory | Same task-local facts and fictional permission limits. The pilot needs no Desk item or runtime native memory. | Use isolated copies of equivalent existing Desk and eligible native memory. Keep normal retrieval and maintenance active in both arms. |
| Room guidance | `off` and `on` mark authored practice examples. | The control admits ordinary context. The candidate selects one current approved room revision by owner, room and native authority and inserts it after authored guidance and before Desk. Both use ordinary context when no eligible revision exists. Scope correction and exclusion to the same governed authority lifecycle. |
| Review and order | Two independent AI-assisted reviewers read one shared randomized packet in its sealed order. | Counterbalance execution order, randomize display with hidden codes and separate the held-out custodian from the scorers. Lock scoring before reveal. Keep owner experience evidence distinguishable from reviewer or scorer output. |

The installed room route, regular context and where the two prompt arms differ were verified separately in Q4-009. Its deterministic replies qualify placement and measurement mechanics. The authored pilot here tests whether the reviewers and objective checks can distinguish work quality and authority failures. ## Workload to agree before 4A

Start the planning discussion with six to eight novel pairs across routine delegated engineering, one consequential product decision, uncertain or missing evidence, and at least one reopen or correction. This is an exploratory starting range for Radzi to accept or revise. Fix the repeats, interactive answer policy, stopping rule, costs, missing-data treatment and material margin in advance. Capture the provider inputs, actual outcome of each artifact check, avoidable questions, justified questions and owner corrections separately. Require a clean no-guidance fallback and a short ordinary conversation that yields eligible evidence without a Desk write. Use the unchanged app separately to quantify any identity or plumbing delta.

The six current practice tasks, their authored variants and every evaluator vector are development-only. Use materially new tasks and private checks after this freeze. Maintain the held-out corpus and evaluator answers away from the learner, tuning and research fixtures. At scoring time the agent receives its task and legitimate evidence. Independent reviewers receive checks and artifacts through a separate route. Re-tuning on an exposed case moves it to development and requires a new untouched case.
