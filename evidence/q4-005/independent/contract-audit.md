Confirm the room before reading and compare current authority at the end. The trusted lower-level read scope checks actor ownership of the starting thread. The current installed format embeds group range endpoints. `MemoryMutationResult` carries operation/configuration/source revisions and a snapshot. It supplies neither an explicit per-group ordered list of eligible messages nor a typed native-message-to-public-turn map. Reflective or discontinuous evidence cannot be inferred from endpoints alone.

Preserve the distinction between native IDs and product identifiers. The selected-context receipt lists selected prior messages and explicitly omits the current input.

A same-text message from a different room contributes no evidence. A committed publication cannot by itself support its prose.

These can sit under the existing operation, thread/resource, authority and configuration fields. Retain app-owned room filtering. A native-to-public-turn reference is additionally needed only for consumers requiring a verified product-transcript anchor. Semantic entailment of generated claims remains an independent check.
