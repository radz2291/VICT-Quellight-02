# Q4-008 · Recovery source audit

The recoverable adapter commits preparation and publication in separate fenced transactions. The point between them is a durable handoff. The product queue owns delivery, and the native operation row owns interpretation of what can safely resume. Product attempt counts and native publication counts measure different events.

## Cross-thread correction controls

Use distinct conversations in the same actor and room with imported native threads. Forget validates selected IDs against **the care command's own conversation**. Forget in B therefore needs a B-native original ID. Correct in B with empty source IDs can advance the shared authority without excluding a specific original. Choose exposure assertions from the eligible native inventory, because a new authority can quarantine derived assistant outputs as well as exclude an explicit message. Native authority mutation takes its own owner-level fence and native authority row lock. Pausing A inside an open prepare transaction can briefly block B. Perform care after the paused process exits and use the confirmed care result and advertised current authority. ## Recommended pin transition

Use a verified drain under the old runtime for native-started work, including prepared and committed-but-unsettled jobs. Establish a single active maintenance writer, inventory nonterminal product jobs against native operation rows, and settle every old-pin operation or retain an explicitly dispositioned blocker before opening candidate claims. Unstarted queued work can continue with the candidate when its product settings, authority and source remain current. Preserve old audit rows and old native observation text. Exact membership for legacy parents is unavailable until a verified current publication supplies it. If a checkpoint cannot be drained safely on its old runtime, a separately authorized transition must preserve it, supersede the old job and enqueue a fresh current-pin logical operation against current eligibility. Switching artifact/hash strings inside an existing row cannot qualify old prepared work. `memory.retry` resets the same blocked job and repeats the same old-pin conflict.
